## Contract and verified mechanisms

Pre-code contract commit: 2cf847f (docs/smoke/s19-stops.sh).
Read PLAN sections 0/S19/5, AGENTS section 8, NAV-AUDIT section 9 and S22 integration.
Followed the Maps/Tesla conventions documented there: next-stop progress, ordered
stop ETAs, auto-continue, and new selections inserted NEXT.

Ferrostar 0.56.0 signatures were inspected in its installed AAR. withProgressView
takes modifier, NavigationUiState and a nullable End callback. TripProgress has
distanceToNextManeuver, distanceRemaining and durationRemaining, no current-step
duration. Current-step remaining duration is total remaining minus the durations
of following steps. Leg boundaries are step endpoints within 50 m of the snapped
waypoint. Remaining geometry starts at the snapped position and current geometry
index. Unresolved boundaries produce Updating trip rather than a final-trip number.

The supplied baseline's RouteCandidate does not store a raw response. The smoke
driver obtains and saves an independent Valhalla multi-leg response, then logs both
its leg summary and TripBar's raw meters/seconds with relative errors.

Stop edits preserve addStop(coordinate, name), insert NEXT, keep existing stops,
and replace under NavLock. A generation/edit guard rejects stale async responses.
Stop arrivals use VoiceGate's arrival class; intermediate generic router arrivals
are suppressed so the named continuation utterance is emitted once per passage.
Intermediate cards dismiss without ending navigation.

TripBar supplies next-leg remaining geometry to Traffic.setRemainingRoute on
route/leg changes and once per minute; empty geometry clears it on disposal/End.
No traffic implementation, PhotonSearch, addingStop block, or settings edits.
